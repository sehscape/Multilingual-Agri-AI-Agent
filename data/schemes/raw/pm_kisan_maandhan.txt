SCHEME: PM-KMY (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana) - Pension for Farmers
SOURCE: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
CATEGORY: Old Age Pension

OVERVIEW
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY) is a voluntary, contributory pension scheme for small and marginal farmers. The farmer pays a small monthly amount until the age of 60, the Central Government pays an equal amount into the farmer's pension account, and after 60 the farmer gets a fixed pension of Rs 3,000 per month for life. The pension fund is managed by the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC).

ELIGIBILITY
- Small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares of cultivable land.
- Age between 18 and 40 years at the time of joining.
- Not eligible: farmers covered under the National Pension Scheme (NPS), ESIC or EPFO schemes; farmers who have joined PM Shram Yogi Maandhan or PM Laghu Vyapari Maandhan; income tax payers; and people holding constitutional posts, serving or retired government officers, ministers and registered professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants).

BENEFIT
- Assured pension of Rs 3,000 per month after the age of 60.
- If the farmer dies after 60, the spouse gets a family pension of 50% (Rs 1,500 per month).
- If the farmer dies before 60, the spouse can continue paying and get the pension, or take out the money with interest.
- The Central Government matches the farmer's contribution rupee for rupee.

MONTHLY CONTRIBUTION
- The farmer's monthly contribution is between Rs 55 and Rs 200, depending on the age at joining.
- Joining at 18 years: Rs 55 per month. Joining at 29 years: Rs 100 per month. Joining at 40 years: Rs 200 per month.
- The contribution is auto-debited from the bank account. A farmer who gets PM-KISAN can choose to pay the contribution from the PM-KISAN money.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Visit the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) with your Aadhaar card and bank passbook. Enrolment is free of charge.
2. The CSC operator fills the form online, links Aadhaar and the bank account, and calculates your monthly contribution.
3. Sign the auto-debit mandate; the first contribution is paid in cash at the CSC.
4. You get a unique Kisan Pension Account Number and a pension card.
5. Farmers can also self-enrol on maandhan.in.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Aadhaar card
- Savings bank account passbook or PM-KISAN account details
- Land records
- Mobile number

IMPORTANT NOTES
- Exit before 10 years: the farmer gets back only their own contribution with savings bank interest.
- Exit after 10 years but before 60: the farmer's own contribution is returned with the interest earned by the fund.
- Missed payments can be regularised by paying the dues with a penalty.
- Joining is voluntary and can be done at any time between 18 and 40 years of age.
