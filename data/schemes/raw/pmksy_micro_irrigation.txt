SCHEME: PMKSY Per Drop More Crop (Drip and Sprinkler Irrigation Subsidy)
SOURCE: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India (Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana)
CATEGORY: Micro Irrigation Subsidy

OVERVIEW
The Per Drop More Crop component of the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) gives farmers a subsidy to install drip irrigation and sprinkler irrigation systems. Drip and sprinkler systems save 30–50% water, reduce fertilizer use when fertilizer is given through drip (fertigation), save labour and increase yield. The scheme is run through the State agriculture and horticulture departments.

BENEFIT
- Small and marginal farmers (up to 2 hectares): 55% of the cost of the drip or sprinkler system is given as subsidy.
- Other farmers: 45% of the cost is given as subsidy.
- The subsidy is calculated on the indicative unit cost fixed for each type and spacing of system. Many states give an extra top-up, so the total subsidy in some states is higher.
- The subsidy is available for up to 5 hectares per farmer.
- Covers drip irrigation, sprinkler irrigation, mini-sprinklers and rain guns as notified by the state.

ELIGIBILITY
- All farmers with agricultural land and a dependable water source.
- Farmers who are members of cooperatives, self-help groups, FPOs and contract farmers on land leased for at least 7 years are also eligible.
- A farmer who has already taken the subsidy on the same land can usually apply again only after 7 years.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Apply online on your state's agriculture/horticulture portal (for example MahaDBT in Maharashtra, the Agriculture Department portal in Haryana and Punjab, the UP Agriculture Department portal) or at the office of the Taluka/Block Agriculture Officer.
2. Choose a drip or sprinkler company registered with the state, and get a quotation and layout design for your field.
3. After pre-sanction, install the system through the registered company.
4. The agriculture officer inspects the installed system, and the subsidy is paid into the farmer's bank account (Direct Benefit Transfer).

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Aadhaar card
- Land records (7/12 extract, khasra-khatauni or jamabandi)
- Bank account passbook
- Proof of water source and electricity connection
- Quotation and field layout from a registered company
- Caste certificate (for SC/ST farmers, where extra benefit applies)
- Soil and water test report where required

IMPORTANT NOTES
- Buy only BIS-marked drip and sprinkler components from a company registered with the state; otherwise the subsidy is not paid.
- Do not install the system before the pre-sanction letter; installations done earlier may not be eligible.
- Since 2022 the Per Drop More Crop component is implemented as part of the Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (RKVY). The subsidy rates above are the central pattern; check your state's current rates.
- Drip is especially useful for sugarcane, banana, cotton, vegetables, chilli and orchards.
