SCHEME: Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
SOURCE: NABARD and Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
CATEGORY: Agricultural Credit / Loan

OVERVIEW
The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) scheme provides short-term credit to farmers for their agricultural operations, post-harvest expenses, maintenance of farm assets, and consumption needs. It was introduced in 1998 and is implemented through commercial banks, regional rural banks (RRBs), and cooperative banks.

PURPOSE
- Purchase of seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and other inputs
- Post-harvest expenses
- Produce marketing expenses
- Consumption needs of farm households
- Maintenance and repair of farm equipment
- Allied activities like animal husbandry, fisheries

ELIGIBILITY
- All farmers (individual or joint borrowers who are owner cultivators)
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees, and sharecroppers
- Self-help groups (SHGs) or joint liability groups (JLGs) of farmers including tenant farmers
- Fishermen, fish farmers, and those engaged in allied activities

CREDIT LIMIT
- Based on the scale of finance for the crop, post-harvest expenses, maintenance charges, and consumption requirements.
- The limit is revised every year with a 10% increase for crop expenses.
- A permanent component is also included for allied activities and term credit.
- Loans up to Rs 3 lakh are available at a concessional interest rate of 7% per annum.
- With interest subvention from the Government, the effective rate can be as low as 4% per annum for timely repayment.

INTEREST RATE
- Up to Rs 3 lakh: 7% per annum (with Government interest subvention of 2%)
- Prompt repayment incentive: Additional 3% subvention — effective rate becomes 4% per annum
- Above Rs 3 lakh: As per the lending bank's rate

REPAYMENT
- Short-term crop loans: Repaid after harvest, typically 12 months.
- The KCC operates as a revolving credit — repay and withdraw again within the sanctioned limit.
- The credit limit is valid for 5 years and reviewed annually.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Visit any branch of a commercial bank, RRB, or cooperative bank.
2. Alternatively apply online via the respective bank's portal or the PM-KISAN portal (farmers registered on PM-KISAN can apply for KCC online).
3. Submit required documents.
4. The bank will issue a KCC (ATM-enabled debit card) after verification.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Identity proof: Aadhaar card / Voter ID / Passport
- Address proof
- Land ownership / tenancy documents (Khasra-Khatauni)
- Passport-size photographs
- No-dues certificate from other financial institutions (for new borrowers)

INSURANCE UNDER KCC
- KCC holders are automatically enrolled under PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) for crop insurance.
- Personal accident insurance of Rs 50,000 for death/permanent disability and Rs 25,000 for partial disability is provided.

IMPORTANT NOTES
- There is no processing fee for KCC loans up to Rs 3 lakh.
- KCC holders get priority for government scheme benefits.
- PM-KISAN beneficiaries can get KCC with simplified procedures.
- The RuPay KCC card can be used at ATMs, PoS machines, and for online transactions.
