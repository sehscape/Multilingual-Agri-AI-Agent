SCHEME: Crop Residue Management (CRM) Scheme - Subsidy on Stubble Management Machines
SOURCE: Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
CATEGORY: Farm Machinery Subsidy / Stubble (Parali) Management

OVERVIEW
The Crop Residue Management scheme helps farmers manage paddy straw (parali) without burning it. It gives a subsidy on machines that mix the straw into the soil or remove it from the field, and supports custom hiring centres so that small farmers can rent these machines. The scheme runs in Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and the National Capital Territory of Delhi, where stubble burning causes severe air pollution.

BENEFIT
- Individual farmers: 50% of the cost of the machine is given as subsidy.
- Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs) set up by cooperative societies, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) and panchayats: 80% of the project cost is given as subsidy.
- Machines covered include Super Straw Management System (Super SMS) attachment for combines, Happy Seeder, Super Seeder, Smart Seeder, Surface Seeder, Zero Till Seed Drill, Mulcher, Paddy Straw Chopper/Shredder/Cutter, Reversible Mould Board Plough, and balers and rakes for collecting straw.
- Support for paddy straw supply chains (collection and supply of straw to biomass power, ethanol and other industries).
- Funds for awareness and training of farmers on in-field and out-of-field straw management.

ELIGIBILITY
- Farmers in Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and NCT of Delhi.
- Cooperative societies, FPOs, registered farmer groups, panchayats and entrepreneurs for custom hiring centres.
- Selection is done by the state through an online application and draw of lots, with priority to small and marginal farmers in many states.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Apply online on your state's agriculture machinery portal when applications open (for example agrimachinerypb.com in Punjab, agriharyana.gov.in in Haryana, the UP Agriculture Department portal in Uttar Pradesh).
2. Select the machine and an empanelled manufacturer or dealer.
3. After approval, buy the machine and submit the bill and machine details.
4. The agriculture department verifies the machine physically, and the subsidy is paid into the bank account.
5. Small farmers can also rent machines from the nearest Custom Hiring Centre; many states run apps to book machines.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Aadhaar card
- Land records (jamabandi / khatauni)
- Bank account passbook
- Tractor registration certificate (for tractor-drawn machines)
- Passport-size photograph and mobile number

IMPORTANT NOTES
- Burning crop residue is banned. Farmers who burn stubble have to pay an environmental compensation fine: Rs 5,000 for less than 2 acres, Rs 10,000 for 2 to 5 acres, and Rs 30,000 for more than 5 acres per incident (as revised in 2024).
- Mixing the straw into the soil (with Happy Seeder, Super Seeder or Mulcher) improves soil organic carbon and saves on fertilizer over time.
- The Bio-decomposer spray (from IARI, Pusa) can also be used to decompose paddy straw in the field in 20–25 days.
