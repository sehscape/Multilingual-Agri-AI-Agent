SCHEME: Soil Health Card Scheme
SOURCE: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India
CATEGORY: Soil Management / Nutrient Management

OVERVIEW
The Soil Health Card (SHC) scheme was launched in February 2015. The Government of India provides every farmer a Soil Health Card once in every 2 years which carries crop-wise recommendations of nutrients and fertilizers required for each farm. This helps farmers improve soil health and productivity while reducing fertilizer costs.

WHAT IS A SOIL HEALTH CARD
A Soil Health Card is a printed report that a farmer gets for each of his fields. It contains the status of 12 soil parameters — primary, secondary, and micro nutrients — along with crop-wise fertilizer recommendations.

SOIL PARAMETERS TESTED
Primary nutrients:
- Nitrogen (N)
- Phosphorus (P)
- Potassium (K)

Secondary nutrients:
- Sulphur (S)

Micro nutrients:
- Zinc (Zn)
- Boron (B)
- Iron (Fe)
- Manganese (Mn)
- Copper (Cu)

Physical properties:
- pH (soil acidity/alkalinity)
- Electrical Conductivity (EC)
- Organic Carbon (OC)

BENEFITS
- Know the exact nutrient status of your soil
- Get crop-specific fertilizer recommendations — avoid over-fertilization
- Reduce fertilizer costs by applying only what is needed
- Improve crop yield and quality
- Detect soil problems like salinity, alkalinity, or acidity early
- Sustainable long-term soil health management

HOW TO GET A SOIL HEALTH CARD
1. Contact your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), State Agriculture Department office, or soil testing laboratory.
2. A government official or agricultural officer will collect a soil sample from your field.
3. The sample is tested at a government soil testing laboratory.
4. The card is prepared and delivered to the farmer within 2 months.
5. Check status online at soilhealth.dac.gov.in.

HOW TO COLLECT SOIL SAMPLES
- Collect soil from 8–10 spots within the same field.
- Dig to a depth of 15–20 cm (6–8 inches).
- Remove surface litter before collecting.
- Mix all samples from the field into one composite sample.
- Label the bag with your name, village, district, and crop details.
- Take samples before sowing or after harvest — avoid sampling immediately after fertilizer application.

INTERPRETING THE CARD
- Green = Sufficient level (no additional input needed)
- Orange = Moderate deficiency (apply recommended dose)
- Red = Severe deficiency (apply higher dose as recommended)

Follow the crop-wise fertilizer recommendations printed on the card for the next two cropping seasons.

LINK WITH OTHER SCHEMES
- KCC holders and PM-KISAN beneficiaries are priority targets for SHC distribution.
- The fertilizer recommendations from SHC can reduce your KCC loan requirement.
- Following SHC recommendations improves eligibility for crop insurance claims.

IMPORTANT NOTES
- The scheme is free of cost for all farmers.
- Cards are issued once in 2 years (every two cropping seasons).
- Each card has a unique 11-digit ID.
- Farmers can view their card history online at soilhealth.dac.gov.in.
