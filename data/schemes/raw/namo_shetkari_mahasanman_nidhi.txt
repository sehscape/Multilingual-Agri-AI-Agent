SCHEME: Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana (Maharashtra)
SOURCE: Department of Agriculture, Government of Maharashtra
CATEGORY: Income Support (State Scheme)

OVERVIEW
Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana is a Maharashtra Government scheme started in 2023. It gives an extra Rs 6,000 per year to farmers in Maharashtra who receive PM-KISAN. Together with the Rs 6,000 from PM-KISAN, an eligible farmer family in Maharashtra gets Rs 12,000 per year as direct income support.

ELIGIBILITY
- Farmer families in Maharashtra who are eligible beneficiaries of PM-KISAN.
- Land records must be updated (land seeding) in the PM-KISAN database.
- e-KYC must be complete and the bank account must be linked to Aadhaar (Aadhaar-seeded, DBT enabled).
- The same exclusions as PM-KISAN apply: income tax payers, government employees, constitutional post holders, and registered professionals are not eligible.

BENEFIT
- Rs 6,000 per year from the Maharashtra Government, in 3 installments of Rs 2,000 each.
- Paid directly into the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account (Direct Benefit Transfer).
- This is in addition to the Rs 6,000 per year of PM-KISAN.

HOW TO APPLY
1. There is no separate application. Farmers who are eligible PM-KISAN beneficiaries in Maharashtra are covered automatically.
2. Make sure PM-KISAN e-KYC is done (on pmkisan.gov.in, the PM-KISAN app with face authentication, or at a CSC).
3. Make sure the bank account is Aadhaar-seeded and DBT is enabled.
4. If the land seeding status shows "No", contact the Talathi or the Taluka Agriculture Officer to update land records.
5. Check payment status on the Namo Shetkari portal (nsmny.mahait.org) using your PM-KISAN registration number or mobile number.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
- Aadhaar card
- Aadhaar-seeded bank account
- 7/12 land record
- PM-KISAN registration number

IMPORTANT NOTES
- If PM-KISAN installments stop because of pending e-KYC or land seeding, Namo Shetkari installments also stop. Fix PM-KISAN issues first.
- Only for farmers in Maharashtra.
- The amount and installment dates are decided by the Government of Maharashtra; check the latest announcement with your Taluka Agriculture Office.
